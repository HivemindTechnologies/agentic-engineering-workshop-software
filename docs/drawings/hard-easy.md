## Ideas

Follow-up to *communication is hard*: what we see in the field is that AI
makes hard things harder and easy things easier — it stretches the poles, it
does not flatten difficulty. Communication is hard and will stay hard.

A large share of the work *is* communication: prioritizing, selecting what
matters, holding a broad picture of the software landscape, business needs,
customer feedback, and more. That is still not an area AI taps into easily.
We have to deliberately bridge that gap ourselves and remind ourselves of it.
