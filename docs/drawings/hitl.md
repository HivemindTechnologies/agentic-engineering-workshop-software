## Ideas

When you are designing a semi-automated process — or you discover you are
already in one — step back and ask what you are optimizing for. Give AI as much
deterministic automation as possible: overall, >90% of the work distribution;
only a tiny rest for the human to inspect.

Data cleansing: you do not want millions of records — only the ones that could
not be resolved automatically. Same for code and PRs: you want to look only at
what really matters. Two angles to shrink that human review surface: improve
the quality of what lands, or reduce how much of it there is. Those are worth
looking at separately next.
